# mackie.world design intent

## Purpose

- A personal reference system for work, writing, experiments, and recurring subjects.
- Audience: engineers, collaborators, and returning readers.
- Primary action: browse the latest items.

## Character

- Practical, curious, unvarnished, modular.
- Bright, useful, unfinished.
- Gridline Workbench: a working catalogue, not a polished marketing archive.

## Durable visual decisions

- Every item reads as a catalogue record, not a card or feature pitch.
- Square, saturated type labels provide the primary visual coordinate.
- Green is a locator; blue indicates interaction.
- Prefer rules, type, and surface changes over shadows, rounded cards, gradients, or decorative effects.
- Site pages stay image-light. Open Graph cards may use the grayscale portrait only as a constrained panel.

## Editorial decisions

- Posts, notes, demos, guides, and projects have equal standing in the public index.
- Topics are canonical terms, not decorative tags.
- Do not expose related-content or backlink UI until the relationships are trustworthy.
- Guides are durable subject hubs; About stays factual rather than résumé-like.
- Projects remain selected external work, not a faux archive.

## Quality bar

- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Preserve public URLs.
- Code owns implementation: tokens, typography, spacing, breakpoints, components, and interaction mechanics live in source files—not here.
