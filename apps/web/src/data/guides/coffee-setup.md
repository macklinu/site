## Start here

This is a structural sample, not a recommendation about a real coffee setup. A guide keeps the decisions around a subject together while allowing its supporting notes and tools to keep their own pages.

## What belongs in a guide

- A short orientation: what the subject is and who the page helps.
- A few stable sections: equipment, recipes, maintenance, and sources.
- Images where a physical detail matters.
- Small components only when they clarify a decision—for example, a coffee-ratio calculator.
